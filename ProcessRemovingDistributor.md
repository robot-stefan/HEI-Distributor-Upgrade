## Removing Existing Distributor
Process to remove the existing distributor. 

> [!Note]
> Complete [Finding Top Dead Center](ProcessFindingTDC.md) before starting this process.

 1. Verify battery is disconnected.
 2. Disconnect spark plug wires.
 3. Disconnect wires running to coil.
 4. Remove bracket holding coil canister. This also required temporary removal of the bracket holding the throttle linkage.
 5. Prior to lifting the unit out of the engine, clean and wipe down area around the distributor. Goal is to clean the area as much as feasible and to reduce risks of dirt or lose debris from getting into the motor. 
 6. Once at TDC[^TDCProc], Remove the distributor hold down clamp using "Box Wrench, Flex Head Ratchet, 9/16 in".
 7. With the clamp removed, you can lift and twist the distributor out. 
 8. Once existing distributor is out, inspect and clean the gasket surface on the engine. If there is gasket material left behind use something such as a plastic pick to scrape away left over gasket material.

 9. Clean the hold down clamp and bolt using a nylon or brass brush. Goal here is to remove debris and inspect threads on the bolt. Always clean fasteners when possible to help identify bad threads and prevent issues that could lead to cross threading, bolt breakage, or improper torquing. 
[^TDCProc]: Process to find TDC is outlined in [Finding Top Dead Center](ProcessFindingTDC.md)
